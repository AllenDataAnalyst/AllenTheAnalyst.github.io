Business Problem

The business needed a clear way to understand customer retention, repeat purchase behavior, and revenue contribution across different customer acquisition periods.

Without cohort analysis, it can be difficult to know whether new customers continue buying, which acquisition periods perform best, and where customer drop off starts happening.

Solution

I created an interactive Power BI dashboard that groups customers into cohorts and tracks their behavior over time. The report helps identify which customer groups are retained, which periods perform best, and where the business may need to improve customer engagement.

Key Features

  • Customer cohort grouping by acquisition period
  • Retention and repeat purchase analysis
  • Revenue trend analysis by cohort
  • Customer behavior monitoring over time
  • Interactive filters for deeper customer insights
  • Clean dashboard layout for business users
