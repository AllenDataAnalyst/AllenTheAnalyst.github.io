Business Problem

Financial data can be difficult to analyze when income statement and balance sheet information are stored in separate tables, spreadsheets, or static reports.

Business users need a clear way to monitor profitability, assets, liabilities, revenue movement, and financial performance without manually reviewing multiple financial files.

Solution

I created an interactive Power BI dashboard that combines income statement and balance sheet data into a clean financial reporting experience. The dashboard allows users to review financial KPIs, analyze business performance, and identify trends across different reporting periods.

Key Features

  • Income Statement reporting view
  • Balance Sheet reporting view
  • Revenue, expense, and profitability analysis
  • Assets, liabilities, and equity monitoring
  • Financial KPI cards and trend visuals
  • Interactive filters for financial review
  • Clean executive style dashboard layout
