Business Problem

Sales data can be difficult to understand when performance is spread across different products, categories, regions, customers, and time periods.

Business users need a clear dashboard that helps them monitor sales performance, identify trends, and understand which areas are driving business results.

Solution

I created an interactive Tableau dashboard that visualizes sales performance, business trends, and key metrics in a clean and easy to understand layout. The dashboard helps users explore data visually and make faster business decisions.

Key Features

  • Interactive dashboard filters
  • Trend analysis over time

  • Clean dashboard design for business users
