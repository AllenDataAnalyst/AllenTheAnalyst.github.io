Business Problem

Airbnb market data can be difficult to understand when listings, prices, bedroom counts, locations, and revenue patterns are spread across a large dataset.

Users need a clear dashboard to understand which areas perform well, how prices vary by location, and what patterns exist across the short term rental market.

Solution

I created an interactive Tableau dashboard that visualizes Airbnb listings, pricing trends, location based performance, and property level insights. The dashboard helps users explore rental market behavior and identify important patterns.

Key Features

  • Airbnb listing performance analysis
  • Average price by location
  • Bedroom and property type comparison
  • Revenue and pricing trend visualization
  • Interactive Tableau filters
  • Location based market insights
  • Clean dashboard layout for business users
